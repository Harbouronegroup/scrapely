Pressure Washing

Bring tired surfaces back to life

Over time, outdoor surfaces collect dirt, moss, algae and grime. They turn dark, look neglected and can become slippery in wet weather.

Our pressure washing removes that build up and restores the appearance of the surface, so your driveway, patio and paths look fresh again.

What we cover

Driveway cleaning

Lift the dirt, tyre marks and green growth that dull a driveway and bring back a clean, welcoming entrance.

Patio cleaning

Restore the colour of your patio slabs and make the space somewhere you want to sit again.

Path cleaning

Clear moss and algae from garden paths and walkways around your property.
