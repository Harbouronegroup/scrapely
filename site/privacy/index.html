Privacy notice

This notice explains how Whispa Cleaning Ltd (“Whispa”) uses personal information sent to us through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, telephone number, and any location, service and message you choose to include.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to arrange and provide our services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Sharing and keeping it

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing. We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting work.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. To do so, call Elizabeth on 07798 656301. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
