About Whispa

Elizabeth'sstandard.

Whispa Cleaning Ltd was established in 2014 and has since made great strides in commercial cleaning. We specialise in the communal areas of residential blocks of flats, and offer a much broader scope of work besides.

Before Whispa

A love of science, and a career in care.

After her GCSEs, Elizabeth went on to study advanced science. She was determined to put it to use, and set her heart on veterinary nursing, taking up a place at a local veterinary hospital.

She trained through the school of nursing while working as a junior team member, gaining her qualifications and rising to become deputy head veterinary nurse of a thriving large and small animal hospital. She worked as a qualified veterinary nurse for nearly fifteen years.

Colleagues used to joke that when she was not treating the animals, she was happiest cleaning the theatre, the patients' area or the consulting rooms.

Founding Whispa

The same skills, a new setting.

When Elizabeth founded Whispa Cleaning in 2014, the skills from her nursing career became fundamental to the company's success: management and business skills, client contact, diligent hard work and, above all, her cleaning skills and knowledge.

Applying them to the cleaning of communal areas in residential blocks came naturally. Today Whispa also works alongside trade and private clients, and looks after the health and safety side of buildings too.

“Whispa, because our cleaning speaks volumes.”
