The Whispa standard

First impressions are made in an entrance hall, on a staircase, in a front room. We look after those spaces carefully, so the people who use them notice only that everything is exactly as it should be.

Whispa Cleaning Ltd was established in 2014. Our speciality is the communal areas of residential blocks of flats, alongside commercial, domestic and one-off cleaning for private and trade clients.

Services

What we look after

  1. For managing agents Communal area cleaning Entrances, stairwells and landings in residential blocks, kept to one dependable standard.
  2. For businesses Commercial & contract cleaning Regular cleaning for workplaces and trade clients, arranged around how you operate.
  3. For homes Home cleaning & decluttering Careful, regular cleaning for private homes, and help clearing the clutter that builds up.
  4. For landlords & homes End of tenancy & one-off cleans A thorough clean between tenants, after building work, or whenever a space needs a fresh start.
  5. For every property Carpet cleaning & floor management Carpets refreshed and hard floors cared for, in homes, workplaces and communal areas.
  6. For managing agents HSE reports & fire alarm testing Health and safety support for the buildings we look after, alongside the cleaning.

Our story

A standard learned in the operating theatre.

Before founding Whispa in 2014, Elizabeth spent nearly fifteen years as a qualified veterinary nurse, rising to deputy head nurse at a busy large and small animal hospital.

When she was not caring for patients, she was cleaning the theatre and consulting rooms. That training, and that eye for detail, now shape every building Whispa looks after.
