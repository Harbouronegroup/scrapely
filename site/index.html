Hello

We are Smoke & Taco

A small street food kitchen with a big smoker, cooking Mexican and L.A. style tacos around Hampshire, Dorset and Wiltshire.

You will usually find us parked up at a pub, a brewery, a food festival or a county show. We also cook for weddings, birthdays and work parties. Have a look at where we are next and come and say hello.

The food

What we cook

Everything is cooked fresh on the day, wherever we have set up. The menu changes a bit from one event to the next, so check Instagram or Facebook to see what is on.

Watch us cook

A few favourites

Slow smoked New Forest brisket. Pulled pork carnitas. Tiger prawn al pastor. Seared sea bass. Loaded birria nachos. And breakfast tacos at some of the shows.

Not everything is on every menu. If you have an allergy, just ask us at the hatch and we will talk you through it.
