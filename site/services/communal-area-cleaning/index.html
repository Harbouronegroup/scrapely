Communal area cleaning

The shared spaces residents walk through every day.

Cleaning the communal areas of residential blocks is what Whispa does best. It has been the heart of the company since 2014.

Request a quoteCall 07798 656301

For managing agents

Across Poole, Bournemouth, Wimborne and the surrounding areas.

An entrance hall is the first thing a resident sees when they come home and the first thing a visitor judges a building by. Whispa looks after those spaces on a regular contract, so the standard holds from one visit to the next.

We work directly with managing agents, building a close working relationship so issues are raised early and the building is always presentable.

What it covers

  • Regular contract cleaning of entrance halls, stairwells, landings and corridors
  • A schedule agreed with the managing agent to suit the building
  • One-off deep cleans when a block needs bringing back up to standard
  • Carpet cleaning and floor care for communal flooring
  • Health and safety support, including HSE reports and fire alarm testing

Every job is agreed with you first, so the scope fits the property.

Why Whispa

Whispa holds approved tradesperson status, so health and safety is designed into every visit. That matters in buildings where residents, visitors and contractors all share the same stairs.
